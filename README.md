> A curated list of resources for traveling in Iceland, covering official tourism portals, road and weather conditions, regional guides, national parks, and safety tools.

Iceland is a dramatic North Atlantic island destination shaped by volcanic fire and glacial ice, featuring geothermal lagoons, black sand beaches, and cascading waterfalls. This list is intended for road-trip travelers, geology enthusiasts, outdoor adventurers, and nature photographers.

## Official & Planning

Essential official government portals, tourist boards, entry guidelines, and mapping tools.

- [Visit Iceland](https://www.visiticeland.com/) - Official national travel guide featuring destination ideas, itinerary suggestions, and regional highlights.
- [Visit Reykjavík](https://visitreykjavik.is/) - Official municipal visitor guide for the capital region, cultural events, and city card info.
- [Icelandic Tourist Board (Ferðamálastofa)](https://www.ferdamalastofa.is/) - Official government agency regulating tourism standards, licensed operators, and travel statistics.
- [Environment Agency of Iceland (Umhverfisstofnun)](https://www.ust.is/) - National authority managing nature reserves, protected areas, and environmental conservation rules.

## Safety, Weather & Road Conditions

Critical real-time safety portals required for driving, hiking, and weather tracking in Iceland.

- [SafeTravel Iceland](https://safetravel.is/) - Official safety initiative by ICE-SAR (Icelandic Association for Search and Rescue) offering trip planning, hazard alerts, and emergency tracking.
- [Umferðin (Vegagerðin)](https://umferdin.is/en) - Official Icelandic Road and Coastal Administration live road status, weather station cameras, and condition map.
- [Icelandic Meteorological Office (Veðurstofa Íslands)](https://www.en.vedur.is/) - Official national meteorological service providing weather forecasts, wind warnings, aurora borealis tracking, and seismic/volcanic alerts.

## Transportation

Navigating rental vehicles, domestic flights, public buses, and ferry networks.

- [Flybus (Reykjavík Excursions)](https://www.re.is/flybus/) - Primary airport shuttle service connecting Keflavík International Airport (KEF) with central Reykjavík.
- [Strætó Public Transit](https://www.straeto.is/en) - National and municipal public bus network operating urban routes in the capital and long-distance lines across regions.
- [Icelandair](https://www.icelandair.com/) - National flag carrier operating international gateways and domestic flights between Reykjavík (RVK) and Akureyri/Egilsstaðir.
- [Baldur Ferry (Saefari / Seatours)](https://www.seatours.is/) - Essential ferry service crossing Breiðafjörður between Stykkishólmur and Brjánslækur, linking the Snæfellsnes Peninsula with the Westfjords.

## Destinations & Regions

### Reykjavík & Capital Region

- [Visit Reykjavík Districts](https://visitreykjavik.is/) - Visitor guide exploring the capital's diverse neighborhoods, culinary scene, museums, and geothermal pools.
- [Perlan Museum & Wonders of Iceland](https://perlan.is/) - Interactive exhibition featuring indoor ice caves, planetarium aurora shows, and observation deck views.

### South Coast & Golden Circle

- [Thingvellir National Park](https://www.thingvellir.is/) - Official visitor portal for the UNESCO World Heritage site, tectonic rift valley, and historic parliament grounds.
- [Vatnajökull National Park](https://www.vatnajokullnationalpark.is/) - Official guide for Europe's largest glacier, Skaftafell hiking trails, and Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon.

### Snæfellsnes Peninsula & West

- [Snæfellsjökull National Park Portal](https://www.ust.is/) - Official administration guide for the glacier-capped volcano and surrounding coastal reserves.

### North & Diamond Circle

- [Visit North Iceland](https://www.northiceland.is/) - Regional tourism hub for Akureyri, Lake Mývatn, Goðafoss, and the Diamond Circle loop.

### East Fjords & Westfjords

- [East Iceland Tourism](https://www.east.is/) - Official guide for remote fjord villages, reindeer habitats, and wilderness hiking routes.
- [Westfjords Tourism](https://www.westfjords.is/) - Visitor portal for exploring dramatic sea cliffs (Látrabjarg), dynjandi waterfall, and isolated coastal roads.

## Geothermal Wellness & Nature

Resources for discovering Iceland's hot springs, geothermal lagoons, and sustainable energy culture.

- [Blue Lagoon Official Portal](https://www.bluelagoon.com/) - Visitor information and booking for the world-renowned geothermal spa on the Reykjanes Peninsula.
- [Sky Lagoon](https://skylagoon.com/) - Oceanfront geothermal infinity spa experience located near central Reykjavík.
